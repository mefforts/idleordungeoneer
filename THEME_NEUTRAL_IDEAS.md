# Fifty theme-neutral mechanic variations

Proposals only; these are separate from the 39 selected themed mechanics implemented in this update. Each variation can use any Zone's materials, creatures and sounds without changing its counterplay. Red/violet communicates danger, teal marks a usable opening or benefit, gold identifies a counter, and blue identifies protection. Shapes and timer arcs carry the rule; detailed words belong in the Codex.

| # | Variation | Behavior and intended counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Delayed Repeat | A strike repeats on the same footprint after a visible pause. Leave, then wait for the second impact before returning. |
| 2 | Narrow Then Wide | A narrow fast line is followed by a wider slow one. Take a small first step and a larger second step. |
| 3 | Advancing Opening | Successive barriers move their opening one position around the arena. Follow the previewed sequence. |
| 4 | Reverse on the Pause | A moving hazard pauses with an opposite arrow, then reverses. Cross during the pause rather than following behind it. |
| 5 | Two Positions Ago | A marked strike targets the player's second-most-recent position. Keep changing direction without immediately returning there. |
| 6 | Last-Second Lock | A target tracks slowly, then visibly locks for the last half-second. Move after the lock. |
| 7 | Deliberate Deceleration | A projectile slows almost to a stop before accelerating again. Read its travel ticks instead of assuming constant speed. |
| 8 | Overshoot and Return | A shot passes a marked destination before turning back along an offset route. Leave room for both paths. |
| 9 | Split at the Marker | One projectile divides into two only when it reaches a visible marker. Cross behind the marker after the split. |
| 10 | Recombine | Two slow projectiles converge into one faster shot. Move out of their displayed convergence line. |
| 11 | Delayed Detachment | A rotating hazard becomes a straight shot after a timer arc empties. Move before the displayed tangent becomes active. |
| 12 | Orbiting Opening | An otherwise continuous ring has one slowly rotating gap. Intercept the gap without chasing it indefinitely. |
| 13 | Expanding Gap | A wall starts with a small non-traversable notch that visibly grows before arrival. Wait for the full opening. |
| 14 | Shrinking Passage | A generous passage narrows after a long countdown. Cross early; the final narrow state is not a surprise. |
| 15 | Alternating Cover | Two cover objects alternate protection with a generous overlap. Move during the overlap. |
| 16 | Limited Cover | Cover absorbs three visible impacts, losing one segment each time. Move before the last segment breaks. |
| 17 | Angled Cover | Cover protects from one previewed direction. Approach its sheltered side rather than standing anywhere nearby. |
| 18 | Moving Cover | A slow protected object crosses an otherwise dangerous lane. Travel alongside it. |
| 19 | Player-Placed Shelter | The first marked position becomes temporary cover for the second attack. Place it thoughtfully before moving. |
| 20 | Close the Circuit | Two nearby markers must be visited in order to interrupt a cast. Their connecting line fills as progress is made. |
| 21 | Carry the Opening | Touching a marker gives a brief protected circle that moves with you. Carry it across one barrier before it expires. |
| 22 | Leave Then Return | A marker activates only after leaving its perimeter. Step out, watch its change, then return. |
| 23 | Counter From the Side | A counter target has a clearly marked one-sided opening. Circle to the opening before making contact. |
| 24 | Counter Relay | A successful counter sends its next target to a marked neighboring object. Follow the visible relay. |

| 27 | Wake With Breaks | A trail has evenly spaced visible breaks. Cross at a break or before activation. |

| 33 | Shared Windup | One enemy prepares while another provides a visible anchor. Interrupt the anchor to shorten the first attack. |
| 34 | Exchanged Roles | Two enemies announce a swap between shield and ranged roles. Change targets when their emblems change. |
| 35 | Relay Protection | A shield moves between three enemies in a previewed order. Attack whichever is currently exposed. |
| 36 | Directional Recoil | A missed attack pushes its caster backward along a marked route. Use positioning to gain a damage window. |
| 37 | Charged Crossing | A mobile enemy charges across one fixed line, pauses, then crosses a second offset line. Evade each separate warning. |
| 38 | Brake Point | A charge stops at a previewed point and creates a short local burst. Avoid both the line and its endpoint. |
| 39 | Flanking Sweep | Two sweep origins attack in sequence, with a clear route between their arcs. Move toward the second origin after the first passes. |
| 40 | Stepwise Rotation | A beam rotates in several discrete, previewed steps rather than continuously. Move only after each lock is shown. |
| 41 | Range Check | A clear inner and outer boundary require staying at medium distance for one strike. Both extremes are marked as dangerous. |

| 44 | Delayed Cross | Two lines activate separately, then briefly together. Use the first line's cleared space before leaving the intersection. |
| 45 | Alternating Sides | A caster alternates left and right attacks, occasionally pausing with both sides previewed. Follow the current silhouette. |
| 46 | Shorten the Hazard | Destroying a weak point reduces a lingering field's duration rather than cancelling it immediately. Buy breathing room without erasing the pattern. |
| 47 | Break One of Three | Three anchors share a hazard, but only one needs to break to open a broad route. Choose the nearest accessible anchor. |
| 48 | Accumulating Exposure | Staying in a field increases its small tick damage gradually, with a visible ring filling on the player. Leave to reset exposure. |
| 49 | Recoverable Mark | A hit places a delayed mark that can be removed at a nearby neutral object. Recover from the first mistake before the follow-up. |
| 50 | Final Clear Lane | A sequence ends with one larger strike whose clear lane matches an earlier opening. Recognize the returning geometry rather than reading a new instruction. |

Use a short, readable warning before the first version; add variations after the basic rule has been learned. Keep mandatory dash checks far enough apart for the normal recharge. Multiple enemies should share a major-attack budget, and simultaneous damage-over-time sources need bounded combined pressure. These ideas should be playtested before any future implementation.
